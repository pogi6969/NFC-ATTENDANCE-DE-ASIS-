How it works

Tap at the gate

The student holds their NFC ID card to the reader when entering or leaving. The first tap of the day is IN, the next is OUT.

Saved in the cloud

The tap goes to the school's database with the exact time and gate. Double taps within a minute are ignored.

Parents are notified

Each parent on file gets a text message, and parents with the app get a phone notification too.

School sees everything

The dashboard shows who is on campus now, late and absent students, daily reports and spreadsheet exports.

Open

Parent app

Activate with the code the school texted you. See when your child entered and left, and turn on notifications.

Gate reader

Turn an Android phone or tablet into the NFC reader at the gate, or connect a USB card reader to a computer.

School dashboard

Students and cards, parents, live attendance, reports, gate readers, text messages and settings.

Works with

Android phone

Chrome on Android reads NFC cards directly. Open the app, choose Gate reader and pair it.

USB NFC reader

Readers that type the card number like a keyboard work in the gate screen. ACR122U readers use the included bridge.

ESP32 + RC522

A low-cost wall reader with a buzzer and lights. The firmware is in the firmware folder.

Any phone for parents

Install the app on Android or iPhone. Parents without the app still get text messages.
